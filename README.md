### 💡 Projects

**1. AI & legal glossary: mapping the regulatory gap** · *best place to start*

A shared glossary of AI and technology terms showing where legal compliance and enforcement lag behind technology.

**Output:** an open, structured glossary (term · technical definition · legal status by jurisdiction · identified gap).

**Contribute:** [ai-legal-glossary](https://github.com/habbine/ai-legal-glossary). Add a term or challenge a definition.

**2. Quantitative legal compliance models**

Mathematical and statistical models that measure how well organisations and technologies comply with legal requirements, so compliance can be scored, compared and improved.

**Output:** an open scoring framework and methodology paper.

**Looking for:** data scientists, applied mathematicians, developers.

**3. Sustainability efficiency measurement under a costs approach**

Building on my published paper on quantitative modeling for sustainability, the [Global Environmental Impact & Efficiency Valuation Model](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4884010), I'm seeking peer review, optimisation and correction of the model.

**Output:** a revised, peer-reviewed model with open calculations.

**Looking for:** econometricians, applied mathematicians, economists.

**4. Neuroscience-inspired legal LLMs for case law analysis**

Comparing how LLMs and the human brain process information, to improve legal LLMs that analyse case law in civil law and common law jurisdictions.

**Output:** an annotated case law dataset (France and common law) and a benchmark for legal LLMs.

**Looking for:** neuroscientists, ML engineers, behavioural scientists, lawyers.
